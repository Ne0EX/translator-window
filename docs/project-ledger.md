# Translumo Local project ledger

This file is the durable release ledger for decisions, known problems, and
verified changes. The detailed investigation history remains in
[implementation-plan.md](implementation-plan.md); model-quality evidence remains in
[local-model/QUALITY.md](../local-model/QUALITY.md). Product terms follow
[CONTEXT.md](../CONTEXT.md).

## Ledger rules

- IDs are permanent and are never reused or renumbered.
- Add a new row when a decision or result is superseded. Link the old ID from the
  new entry instead of deleting history.
- `Accepted`, `Rejected`, `Deferred`, and `Open` describe the current disposition.
- Paths under `.cache/` or `artifacts/` are ignored, machine-local evidence. They
  are useful for reproduction on the development machine and are not part of the
  repository or release. Private reader images and user screenshots are never
  published.
- Measurements describe the stated Windows laptop and fixture. They are evidence,
  not performance guarantees for other machines or pages.
- Dates record when this ledger baseline was reconstructed from the source tree and
  investigation record. They do not claim the original implementation date unless
  the linked detailed history says so.

| PROB-001 | 2026-09-25 | Open | Source artwork can still stutter during scrolling and paper-like page turns, even after captions finish. | Real-model traces reproduced multi-second source callback gaps under severe memory pressure. WPF callbacks are not physical presentation measurements. | In an app built from the v0.1.0 source, manually compare mouse-wheel and two-finger touchpad navigation with the new 450 ms scoped pause. | PROB-002 | 2026-09-25 | Open | Cold first-caption and dense-page completion remain several seconds and can become much worse under pressure. | Representative Release runs remained well above the one-second working target. | PROB-003 | 2026-09-25 | Open | The 14 GB host and 6 GB GPU can approach paging and GPU-memory limits with OCR and translation resident. | Q8 KV saves 224–226 MiB of native dedicated GPU memory; FP16 OCR saved about 639 MiB. | PROB-006 | 2026-09-25 | Open | Tiny chart captions may move to numbered margins instead of fitting inside their source boxes. | Full-page verification renders all 30 page-10 captions with 10 association badges and all 24 page-21 captions with 1 badge.
